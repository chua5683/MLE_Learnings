## How to use it

1. **Self-quiz mode**: cover the answer and answer out loud; if you stall for more than 30 seconds, write the question down.
2. **Chain mode**: draw three random questions and connect them with one thread (e.g., "class imbalance" → "AUC vs PR" → "thresholds and business cost" → "calibration").
3. **Mistake log**: paste missed questions back into the relevant chapter with the date. Missing the same question twice means a gap in understanding — go back to the derivation.

## The 90 minutes before an interview

- 30 min: skim the cheat sheet for your track (ML/DL or LLM/Agent).
- 30 min: review the "pitfalls" in the top 100 questions.
- 15 min: review your project deep-dive notes.
- 15 min: review the system design template and write the answering framework from memory.

> Do not learn anything new the night before. Sleep improves your pass rate more than 20 extra questions.
